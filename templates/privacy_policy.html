Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Voyagr Navigation ("Voyagr", "we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

Who we are (Data Controller)

{% if privacy_operator_name %}{{ privacy_operator_name }} operates Voyagr Navigation and is the data controller.{% else %}The operator of Voyagr Navigation is responsible for personal data processed through the app. Its public legal identity has not been configured for this deployment.{% endif %} {% if privacy_contact_email %}For privacy questions and rights requests, contact {{ privacy_contact_email }}. {% else %}A public privacy-contact email has not been configured for this deployment. {% endif %}

1. Data We Collect

  • Location data — When you use GPS tracking or navigation, your device's location is used to provide turn-by-turn directions, speed monitoring, and route progress. Coordinates may be sent to Voyagr and relevant service providers for road-context, routing and nearby-place lookups. Coordinates may also be retained in route caches, saved trips, dashcam metadata and community reports, depending on the features you use.
  • Route calculations — Start and end coordinates are sent to routing services to calculate routes. These are processed for the requested journey. New temporary route-cache entries use private server memory with expiry configured within 15 minutes; they are not written to the server route-cache database. Older database cache records may remain pending separate deletion. Saved trips and account synchronisation are separate features.
  • Saved routes, places and history — This version stores saved routes and places in this browser's IndexedDB, separated into guest and signed-in device profiles. Journey and recent-search recording starts off and can be enabled in Settings. Current-route recovery is separate from history. Older unassigned device data is preserved for explicit recovery or deletion.
  • Dashcam recordings (only if you start recording) — This version commits recording chunks to this browser's IndexedDB. Audio starts off; optional local GPS metadata requires its separate recording control. New recordings are not automatically uploaded when recording stops or you sign in. Interrupted recordings may contain recoverable partial media. Existing recordings previously uploaded remain on the server until separately deleted.
  • Community reports — Reports you submit include a location, hazard type and any description you provide, and may be shown to other users. Active expiry controls display; deletion of expired records depends on cleanup. Do not include names, contact details or other identifying information in reports.
  • Account data — If you sign in, your email address and display name are managed by Supabase (our authentication provider).
  • Preferences — Structured preferences are stored in IndexedDB. Existing settings controls also use localStorage as a compatibility projection. Authentication tokens are managed separately by the login SDK.
  • Optional cloud sync — New personal-data synchronisation and dashcam backup are unavailable during this privacy upgrade. Signing in does not enable uploads. Necessary routing, traffic and lookup requests are separate from backup and still leave your device when needed. Existing server data is not erased by this change.

Device storage and recovery

Use Settings > On this device to export structured data or delete a category. Recording media is downloaded separately; the structured export includes its metadata. Exports and browser storage are not encrypted by this app. Browser clearing, eviction, device loss or private-browsing limits can remove device data; keep your own protected copies of important recordings and exports. Local deletion does not erase cloud copies, downloaded files or external backups. There is no automatic restoration of an exported backup in this version.

Voice and audio controls

Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser vendor's recognition service. If you choose AI speech, a recording of up to 15 seconds is sent through Voyagr to the deployment's configured transcription service. Voyagr's transcription handler processes the recording in memory and does not save an audio file; any upstream provider retention depends on that provider's terms and configuration. Recognised commands, and your current coordinates when available, are sent to Voyagr to carry out the command.

If enabled and configured, the wake-word feature listens to microphone audio locally while its browser pipeline is active. It uses Picovoice software and may require vendor licence validation; it is not a promise of entirely offline use. The microphone check opens and releases capture without uploading an audio clip. Device choices stay in page memory. You can stop voice input, disable wake listening, or revoke microphone permission in browser settings. Dashcam audio is separate.

Optional analytics

Optional Google Analytics is off until you choose Allow analytics below. Declining does not prevent navigation. Your choice is stored in this browser separately from the safety acknowledgement and account settings. You can change it here at any time. When allowed, Google may process usage events, online identifiers and network data under its own policy. Voyagr's page-view event omits the page query string and fragment and uses a generic page title. This does not make analytics anonymous.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers for their own marketing.
  • Advertising or cross-site tracking is not required to navigate. Optional analytics is described above.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, photo library, or other existing files on your device. Dashcam creates new recordings only when you choose to record; it does not browse or upload unrelated media from your device.
  • We do not start microphone or camera capture merely to load the app. Optional voice commands, wake listening, microphone checks and dashcam have separate controls and require the relevant browser/device permissions.

3. Third-Party Services

Voyagr uses the following third-party services that may process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • TomTom — Traffic data and geocoding. TomTom Privacy Policy
  • Esri and imagery contributors — Satellite imagery when that map theme is selected. Tile requests reveal the viewed map area and network information to the imagery service. Esri Privacy Statement
  • OpenWeatherMap — Weather conditions along routes. OpenWeatherMap Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — User authentication (if you sign in). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap — Map tiles and geographic data. OSM Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics (only if allowed) — Optional usage statistics, online identifiers and network data to understand how the app is used. Google Privacy Policy

Where these providers process personal data on our behalf (for example our authentication and hosting providers), they act as our processors under appropriate data protection terms.

4. Lawful Bases for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Performance of a contract / providing the service you request — e.g. calculating a route, showing your position, or saving a trip you ask us to save.
  • Consent — e.g. accessing your device location; accessing the camera and/or microphone for dashcam; saving dashcam recordings and related GPS metadata you choose to create; or creating and syncing an account. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Location Permission, Dashcam Permissions, and Your Rights). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out while consent was in place.
  • Legitimate interests — e.g. keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and maintaining reliability, balanced against your rights.

5. Location Permission

Voyagr uses location access for requested location features such as GPS tracking, navigation and current-location searches, including enabled automatic-location preferences. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device or browser settings. The app functions without location access (route planning, cost estimation) but real-time navigation features require it. If you record with dashcam while location is available, GPS points may be stored with that recording as described below.

6. Dashcam Permissions and Saving Recordings

Dashcam is an optional feature. Before any dashcam capture begins, your browser or device will ask for permission to use the camera and, if audio is enabled, the microphone. We rely on your consent for this processing under UK GDPR.

  • When permissions are requested — Camera (and microphone, if audio recording is on) access is requested when you start a dashcam recording, not merely because the Dashcam tab is open.
  • What is saved — New recording media and any enabled GPS metadata stay in this browser. You can play, download and delete device recordings. Managing older uploaded recordings requires authentication and remains separate. Denying camera permission prevents recording.
  • Purpose — To provide the dashcam service you request (reviewing a journey, incident evidence for your own use, and related playback or cleanup in the app). We do not use dashcam footage for advertising or sell it to third parties.
  • Your control — You can turn audio recording off in Dashcam settings, choose an age for explicit cleanup (for example 7, 14, 30, or 90 days), delete individual recordings, or run cleanup of older recordings in the app. You can also revoke camera and microphone access at any time in your browser or device settings; after revocation, new recordings cannot be created until you grant permission again.
  • Special category / sensitive content — Dashcam footage may incidentally capture other people, vehicle registration marks, or conversations. You are responsible for using dashcam lawfully (including any local rules on recording in public or in vehicles) and for deciding what you record and keep.

7. Data Storage, Retention and Security

  • Preferences and recent destinations are stored locally in your browser and remain on your device until you clear site data (for signed-in users, settings and saved routes may also sync to your account — see below).
  • Trip history you save while signed in is stored in a server-side database linked to your account.
  • Dashcam storage: Saved dashcam video files and associated GPS metadata are stored on our servers so you can play back, manage, and delete them in the app. Transfer uses HTTPS.
  • All communication uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Retention: Saved trips and account data remain until deletion is performed. Data held only in your browser stays until you clear it. Server-side route-cache entries now expire in memory within 15 minutes under normal operation. Older database cache records are not automatically erased by this change; legacy freshness checks do not guarantee their deletion. A secure, short-lived cookie separates your browser's temporary route cache; it is not used for analytics or account identification and expires after 15 minutes. Dashcam's retention setting determines which recordings its cleanup operation removes. You can delete individual recordings and saved trips using their controls. These controls do not by themselves erase account-provider data, operational logs or backups.
  • Contact the operator to request access, deletion or details of deployment-specific retention and backup periods. Those periods must be established by the operator; this policy does not promise an unverified automatic purge.

8. International Data Transfers

Some of our providers (for example our authentication and hosting providers) may store or process data on servers located outside the UK or European Economic Area. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards — such as UK/EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision — so that your data receives an equivalent level of protection.

9. Children's Privacy

Voyagr is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data (including dashcam recordings and related GPS metadata saved through the app)
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent for location tracking, camera/microphone access, saving new dashcam recordings, or account sync at any time
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

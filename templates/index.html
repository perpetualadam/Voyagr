Before you drive

Voyagr is a navigation aid only — not an official or certified navigation system.

By continuing, you agree that:

  • You use Voyagr at your own risk. Maps, routes, traffic, speed limits, and voice guidance may be inaccurate, incomplete, or delayed.
  • You must obey all road signs, signals, and applicable traffic laws at all times. Nothing shown in the app overrides them.
  • You remain solely responsible for safe driving, your vehicle, passengers, and compliance with restrictions (speed, weight, height, emissions zones, and tolls).
  • You will only use the phone in ways that are safe and lawful (e.g. hands-free where required, or when stopped).

Privacy at a glance

  • Location — used only when you allow it, for navigation, speed help, and your position on the map.
  • Dashcam (optional) — camera and microphone are used only if you start recording and grant permission. Video, optional audio, and GPS metadata for that session may be saved on our servers until you delete them or they expire under your retention setting.
  • On your device — settings, recent places, and cached maps stay in your browser unless you sign in.
  • On our servers — route requests are processed to calculate journeys; saved trips and account data are stored only if you sign in. Optional dashcam recordings you create are also stored so you can play them back. We do not sell your data.
  • Partners — map, traffic, weather, and sign-in providers process data under their own policies.

Lane and legal-speed presentation use one current road snapshot and source revision. Browser request parameters cannot mark an OSM way trusted. Source context contains server-generated directional bundles using Phase 6 `lane_evidence` / `legal_speed_evidence`; browser consumption checks source revision, selected road, direction, vehicle and current match freshness. Old route/vehicle requests cannot replace current identity. No source or ambiguous matching means no definitive lane/legal value.

Lanes retain EXPLICIT/PROVIDER/INFERRED/UNKNOWN. Explicit choices require applicable matched directional turn tags, counts/access and understood manoeuvre. All legal shared choices remain. Provider-active lanes require a typed source match to their route binding; geometry-compatible alone cannot promote provider lane identity. Provider adapters usually lack such IDs, so their lanes are conservatively suppressed until binding exists. Ordinary turn guidance remains. No roundabout lane permission is invented from a generic turn.

Speed retains MAPPED_POSTED/MAPPED_CONDITIONAL/LEGAL_RULE_INFERRED/CONFLICTING/UNKNOWN. Numeric mapped values require appropriate direction, jurisdiction, vehicle and ≤300 s source observation age. Bare OSM numeric values remain km/h, explicit mph remains mph. Traffic-flow/route/default/GPS speed never becomes law. Car petrol/diesel selection maps explicitly to car; motorcycle is supported. Van/truck, ambiguous electric class, bicycle/pedestrian and unsupported contexts cannot inherit car legal values. National, conditional, temporary/variable and heavy-vehicle legal interpretation remain unverified. GPS speed display/preferences and explicit strict-limit threshold checks remain.

Camera/hazard association requires compatible OSM way IDs or graph IDs in the same provider namespace, fresh match and hazard evidence, bounded positional uncertainty and applicable direction. Explicit unrelated/opposite-carriageway evidence can suppress an alert; missing/stale IDs or average-speed section membership remains POSSIBLE. Existing route/nearby proximity collection remains. Possible alerts say road association is uncertain, including former definitive route-camera wording. Separate mandatory evaluator requires fresh namespaced identity before clearing; geographic avoid-points remain geographic constraints. Existing synthetic avoidance/multi-stop tests now explicitly supply their namespace and freshness; their VIOLATED/NO_COMPLIANT_ROUTE and automatic-selection prohibitions remain unchanged.

Known limitations: most real hazard records currently lack complete road/direction/freshness/section metadata and therefore remain possible. Source timestamps are acquisition observations, not guarantees signs stayed unchanged. Source coverage, source access restrictions and operator review are mandatory. The loader refuses SYNTHETIC data as a live authority; the acquisition tool outputs PARTIAL rather than approving its own coverage. A reviewer must establish completeness and legal applicability before enabling any source in deployment. No production dataset/configuration was changed.

## Exclusion parity finding

GraphHopper's custom-model camera areas and Valhalla's excluded points are different mechanisms. Fifty sampled points cannot establish exclusion of every segment crossing a mandatory area. A clear route in one fixture proves neither universal enforcement nor no-compliant-route refusal. Current endpoint-in-area checks and hazard matching do not, by themselves, establish complete polygon-interior intersection coverage for Valhalla. **Full-area enforcement/coverage and adversarial no-compliant-route behavior remain release blockers.** This change adds no fallback permission and makes no production claim that those blockers are resolved.

For each mandatory category (camera areas, camera points, other hazards, road/segment bans, railway crossings), require complete fresh independently bound source coverage plus full returned-segment verification. Unsupported requirements must leave navigation refused. This applies to manual and automatic navigation for every enabled normalized mandatory category: violated, unverified, unsupported, missing/malformed or stale/snapshot-mismatched evidence cannot be confirmed into permission. This policy supersedes the earlier Phase 4 explicit-acceptance wording in `VOYAGR_ROUTING_AVOIDANCE_INTEGRITY.md`; unresolved preference categories remain best effort. Refused routes may remain previews. Toll/motorway preference tests should measure preference behavior separately from hard-ban tests. Preserve failed and relaxed-recovery captures: success after dropping an exclusion cannot satisfy the original request.

## Reuse and adapter boundary

Reuse `road_oracle.py`, its exact endpoint-segment specifications, unresolved-restriction safeguards, candidate validation and private inspectors; `motorcar_investigation.py` remains diagnostic. Reuse `origin_integrity`, `journey_geometry`, common HTTP/cache integrity and browser tests. Existing OSM tests cover directed segments, barriers, conditional access and unsupported physical rules. No exporter or road-search semantics changed.

The new `valhalla_acceptance.py` is a wire-binding/geometry adapter, not an engine or legality oracle. It accepts five auto-costing captures, polyline6, kilometer summaries, up to 20 locations/legs and two alternatives, with finite bounded data. It checks all supplied legs/alternatives, ordered geometric waypoint visits and the existing 2m endpoint gate. It does not validate request exclusion semantics, all costing options, maneuver instruction meaning, service at stops, native directed edges or imported OSM restriction completeness. Other costing/shape formats are refused pending version-specific adapters.

Wrong-carriageway displacement can be detected when geometry is more than 2m away. A nearby parallel road within tolerance, reverse travel on an overlapping shape, legal turn, barrier, vehicle dimensions and conditional rules cannot be established geometrically. All remain `UNVERIFIED`, including perfectly continuous synthetic shapes. Native snap support must wait for authentic route-bound records from the installed build; no inferred snap is promoted to provider evidence.

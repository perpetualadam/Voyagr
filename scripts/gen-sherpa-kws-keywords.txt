Regenerate static/vendor/sherpa-kws/spike-config/keywords-hey-sat-nav.txt
==============================================================

1) Download and extract:
   sherpa-onnx-kws-zipformer-zh-en-3M-2025-12-20
   from https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/kws-models

2) Create keywords_raw.txt (one phrase per line; spaces in phrase, underscores in @tag):
   HEY SAT NAV @HEY_SAT_NAV

3) Install tools:
   pip install sherpa-onnx sentencepiece pypinyin

4) Run (paths adjusted to your extract folder):
   sherpa-onnx-cli text2token ^
     --tokens sherpa-onnx-kws-zipformer-zh-en-3M-2025-12-20/tokens.txt ^
     --tokens-type phone+ppinyin ^
     --lexicon sherpa-onnx-kws-zipformer-zh-en-3M-2025-12-20/en.phone ^
     keywords_raw.txt keywords.txt

5) Optionally append boosting / threshold to the token line, e.g. :1.3 #0.28 before @HEY_SAT_NAV

6) Copy keywords.txt into Voyagr as:
   static/vendor/sherpa-kws/spike-config/keywords-hey-sat-nav.txt
